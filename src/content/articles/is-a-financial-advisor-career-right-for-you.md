## Transferable experience

Potentially relevant backgrounds may include sales, banking, client service, relationship management, customer support, entrepreneurship, and leadership. Final selection remains subject to the authorized recruitment process.

## Questions to ask yourself

- Am I comfortable building professional relationships?
- Am I willing to learn continuously?
- Can I communicate clearly and responsibly?
- Am I prepared for goals, accountability, and self-management?
- Do I want to develop leadership skills over time?
