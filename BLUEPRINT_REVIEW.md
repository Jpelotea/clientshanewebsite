# Blueprint Review

## Approved direction

The website is Shane Perez's personal professional platform. It is not an official Manulife Philippines, branch, or corporate website.

### Brand
- Public name: Shane Perez
- Full formal name: Shane Villasor Perez where appropriate
- Signature positioning: Builder of Builders
- Descriptor: Branch Head, Leadership Mentor, and Financial-Services Professional
- Core message: Shane develops professionals who can become leaders, mentors, and builders of other leaders.

### Connected goals
1. Personal brand and leadership authority
2. Recruitment and career opportunities
3. Financial education and consultation

### Primary audiences
- Aspiring Financial Advisors
- Existing Financial Advisors
- Aspiring Unit Heads and future leaders
- Experienced professionals considering career transition or leadership

### Sitemap
Home; About Shane; Leadership Journey; Builder of Builders; Join My Team; Team and Leadership Community; Financial Education; Insights and article pages; Success Stories; Events and Achievements; Book a Consultation; Recruitment Application; Contact; Privacy Policy; Cookie Notice; Terms and Disclosures; Custom 404.

### Primary conversion paths
- Career exploration → application
- Trust-building content → consultation request
- Leadership story → career or leadership inquiry

### Calls to action
- Explore Career Opportunities
- Apply to Join the Team
- Request a Consultation
- Learn About Shane
- Send a Message

### Content controls
All claims, statistics, credentials, testimonials, photos, addresses, and company-specific wording require verification and approval. Draft content remains labeled and hidden from normal production listings.

### Technical direction
Astro, Netlify, Decap CMS, private résumé storage, server-side form validation, Turnstile, consent-gated analytics, and modular static components.

### Maintenance direction
Shane owns accounts. Her authorized team can edit approved structured content through Decap CMS. Technical maintenance excludes regular copywriting, new pages, and new integrations unless separately scoped.
